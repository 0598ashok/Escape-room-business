Attorney Biography

A Career Built on Principled Advocacy

Jonathan M. Hargrove, Esq. graduated summa cum laude from Columbia Law School in 2000, where he served as Editor-in-Chief of the Columbia Law Review. He began his legal career as an Assistant District Attorney for New York County before transitioning to private practice, where he discovered his calling: championing families through their most difficult moments.

In 2003, Jonathan founded LexCounsel with a singular vision — to build a boutique firm where every client receives the unwavering attention and tenacious advocacy they deserve. Today, LexCounsel is recognized as one of New York's premier family and estate law practices.

"Every case I take is personal. I don't just represent clients — I stand beside them."
