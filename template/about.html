The Architect

A Vision for Immersive Storytelling

Alex Thorne graduated from the Rhode Island School of Design with a background in theatrical set design and mechanical engineering. After years designing special effects for off-Broadway productions, Alex saw an opportunity to bring high-production value and narrative depth to the emerging world of escape rooms.

In 2018, Alex opened VaultEscape in a converted Brooklyn warehouse. What started as a small experimental project has grown into a world-class facility known for its "logic-first" puzzle design and cinematic atmosphere.

"We don't just build rooms — we build worlds that challenge the mind and soul."
